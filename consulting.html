Consulting

Consulting

Independent analysis of energy markets, prices and regulation for Pakistan, the United States and global markets.

The institute takes on advisory work built on the same methods as its published research and market models. Engagements are scoped to the question at hand and delivered as written analysis, model results or briefings.

  • Market and price analysis

    Analysis of wholesale power, natural gas, LNG, oil and coal markets: supply and demand balances, price drivers, contract structures and the effect of policy or disruption on prices and flows.

    Pakistan · United States · Global

    Related research

  • Power and gas modelling

    Scenario modelling of power systems and gas supply using documented, open-source methods: zonal and hub prices, capacity, generation mix, load growth and gas balances, with assumptions set out in full.

    United States and Canada power zones · North American and global gas

    Market modelling programme

  • Policy and regulatory analysis

    Review of tariff design, cost recovery, circular debt, interconnection and transmission rules, capacity markets, net metering and planning frameworks, read against published regulatory filings and evidence.

    Pakistan · United States · Global

    Pakistan research · United States research

  • Custom research

    Commissioned papers and briefing notes on a defined energy question, prepared to the institute’s research standards and grounded in public data and cited sources.

    Any market the institute covers

    Joint papers and methods review
