Models

Open cases in progress

The institute is building its own open production-cost style power cases and a cited gas price and balance view so that published research can rest on numbers the institute can rebuild, audit, and revise when the evidence changes.

Commercial datasets and black-box forecasts are useful for orientation. They are a poor foundation for work that asks whether tariffs recover cost, whether capacity payments match the load that arrives, or whether an LNG destination swings because of price, security, or both. The modelling programme exists so those questions can be answered from public inputs, with every non-blank cell carrying a citation, and with blank cells left blank when an open source is not yet in hand.

Nothing on this page is a published price forecast. The cases describe method and limits. Where a solve has returned optimal, that result is an energy-balance check under stated assumptions, not a locational market price.

Power

Zonal production-cost cases

The power programme is organised as open production-cost style cases for North American wholesale footprints. PJM and NYISO are the first markets taken through a full copper-plate dispatch. The generator fleets for those markets are drawn from the EIA-860 2024 release, filtered to the relevant balancing authority and using operable nameplate capacity as the cited installed capacity. For PJM that yields on the order of 3,800 units and roughly 224 GW of nameplate; for NYISO roughly 1,900 units and about 46 GW. Zone assignment and marginal cost fields on those fleets are still blank. Plants have not been mapped to PJM transmission zones or to NYISO load zones A through K from a cited crosswalk, and the weights used in the first solves are not fuel costs.

Load samples for the first checks are recent public hourly series: EIA-930 subregion hours for PJM and NYISO market information system integrated load for zones A through K. The copper-plate runs treat the balancing authority as a single bus. Zonal loads are summed, carriers are aggregated by technology with the sum of cited nameplate, and a linear programme (PyPSA with the HiGHS solver) balances generation against that aggregate load across 168 hourly snapshots. Equal dummy weights are applied on every carrier solely so the solver has an objective. That is not a fuel-price stack. Locational marginal prices and duals are neither computed nor written.

Under those assumptions the PJM and NYISO copper-plate solves have returned optimal, with peak demand in the sample week on the order of 102 GW in PJM and 18 GW in NYISO and with imbalance at machine precision. Similar copper-plate energy-balance checks have also returned optimal where comparable EIA-860 fleets and EIA-930 load samples are already in place for ERCOT and SPP, and in the MISO and ISO-NE folders where load samples and BA fleets have been attached. CAISO and the wider Western interconnect remain at zone stubs without a priced or energy-balance solve. Canadian footprints (AESO, IESO, and the hydro provinces) sit as structured stubs pending the same open-input discipline.

A priced zonal case is not yet available. The blocking gaps are deliberate rather than temporary inventiveness. Plants must be mapped to zones from a cited source. Marginal costs must come from published fuel prices and heat rates, or from another documented open cost series. Interface limits between zones must be introduced before transmission constraints mean anything. The fleet year (EIA-860 2024) and the load sample year (September 2026 hours in the current extracts) still need to be aligned for a study year the institute will stand behind. For PJM, the OVEC load series remains absent from the EIA-930 subregion extract used so far. Until those pieces are cited and attached, the institute will not publish locational prices from these cases.

Gas

Benchmark prices and balance history

The gas programme assembles open benchmark prices and regional balance history. It does not invent hub series that are not free to redistribute under their own names, and it does not fill regional volumes from proxies.

United States Henry Hub daily spots from the EIA are in through 22 September 2026. World Bank Pink Sheet monthly series supply the first non-United States price history: Europe natural gas from January 1960 through August 2026, and Japan LNG from January 1977 through August 2026, drawn from the Pink Sheet workbook updated in early September 2026. Those Europe and Japan series are the World Bank commodity markers. They are not TTF and not JKM. Named TTF and JKM spot columns remain intentionally empty. They will stay empty until an open free series under those names is obtained. Eurostat and other non-United States annual balance volumes are likewise blank on purpose; earlier stubs are not estimates.

Alongside the price extracts, North American supply tables, United States citygate monthly averages, Canadian production from the Canada Energy Regulator, and a North America global-balance roll-up for United States production, consumption, and LNG exports are in the processed set. Hub basis series for Waha, AECO, Dawn, Dom South, and Chicago are not filled. Net imports and LNG import splits for North America that would require an undocumented pipe versus LNG allocation are left blank. Mexico supply volumes and Canada consumption and trade remain open. No forecast price or volume appears without a model run or a citation.

Sequence

What is not in the cases yet

The next work is ordered so that each layer rests on cited inputs rather than on a completed narrative. Zonal power prices come only after plant-to-zone mapping and fuel costs exist for the lead markets. Capacity, renewable credit, and revenue views are intended to be built from those modelled prices once they exist; the capacity folders are scaffolded and do not yet hold published outputs. Transmission detail will enter from public power-flow cases, not from invented interface ratings.

On the gas side, the open queue is annual volumes for Europe and other regions from Eurostat and JODI when those extracts land, and named TTF and JKM spots only when a free open series under those labels appears. Basis and non-North America balances stay blank until the same standard is met.

Readers should treat this page as a statement of method and of known limits. No price forecast from this programme is published yet. When a priced case clears under documented assumptions, the institute will say so in the research archive with the same sourcing standard used elsewhere on the site.
