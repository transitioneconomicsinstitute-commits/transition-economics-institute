Collaborate

Researchers and institutions are invited to work with the institute on energy-market analysis grounded in cash-flow, contracts, and published evidence.

  • Joint papers

    Co-authored analysis where both parties share the evidence base and the operational framing.

  • Methods review

    Critical review of data, models, and planning assumptions before a paper or programme advances.

  • Seminars

    Focused exchanges for practitioners who need the cash-flow and contract view of the transition.
